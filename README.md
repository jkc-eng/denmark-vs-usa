# Denmark vs. the U.S.

An interactive comparison of Denmark and the United States for students of American Studies at Aarhus University.

**Open the app:** https://jkc-eng.github.io/denmark-vs-usa/

By Jens Kjeldgaard-Christiansen, Department of English and American Studies Center Aarhus, Aarhus University. Photo and clip credits, with each item’s license, are listed inside the app.
